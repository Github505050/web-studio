WHAT I ACTUALLY FIX

Problems clients notice — and problems hidden underneath.

A visible glitch is usually only the symptom. The real work is tracing the cause and repairing the component in a way that stays stable after release.

01

Broken state & calculations

Pricing totals, counters, tabs, filters, toggles and other interactions that look fine at first but produce stale or incorrect output.

02

Forms that confuse users

Validation gaps, unclear errors, disabled submit flows, and forms that fail silently or provide no usable feedback.

03

Responsive regressions

Layouts and components that break on smaller screens, create overflow, or become difficult to tap and navigate.

04

Accessibility & interface reliability

Unsynchronised ARIA state, focus issues, weak contrast, and interface patterns that frustrate keyboard and assistive-technology users.

HOW I APPROACH REPAIRS

Fast diagnosis is useful. Stable fixes are better.

Whether the issue lives in CSS, JavaScript, or a component’s structure, the goal is the same: fix the cause, not just the symptom.

Reproduce accurately

Device, browser, steps, expected outcome, actual outcome, and any edge cases that make the issue easier to isolate.

Fix the underlying logic

Adjust markup, state, events, or styling rules in a way that remains understandable when someone maintains the code later.

Retest the affected flow

Verify the repaired behaviour at the impacted screen sizes and interaction paths so the regression does not simply move elsewhere.
