QUESTIONS

About landing page work

The examples are fictional exercises. A real project would use the client’s approved branding, copy and assets.

The sample pages include working section links and distinct interactive controls where relevant. Demo-only call-to-action buttons do not pretend to submit private messages.

The layout, type, navigation, spacing, images and interactive controls need to work at common phone, tablet and desktop widths.
