02 / ENGINEERED FOR CLARITY

What makes a support interface usable?

Good support design starts with accurate source content and understandable interaction—not a chatbot that claims to answer everything.

01

Approved knowledge

Organise business policies, common questions and answers into a content structure that can be reviewed and updated.

02

Intent + safe fallback

Match the user's question to supported topics. When confidence is low, invite another question rather than inventing an answer.

03

Comfortable interaction

Quick suggestions, keyboard-friendly input, reset controls and responsive conversation design reduce friction.
